## First Class Levers:
![First Class Lever](1st%20class%20lever.png)

(_Formulas:_)

IMA = Length of effort arm divided by length of resistance arm &rarr; D<sub>effort</sub>/D<sub>load</sub>

AMA = Force of resistance divided by force of effort &rarr; F<sub>load</sub>/F<sub>effort</sub>

Ideal Effort Force = Multiply the load force by the load distance, then divide by the effort distance &rarr; (F<sub>resistance</sub>&middot;D<sub>resistance</sub>)/D<sub>effort</sub>

## Second Class Levers:
![Second Class Lever](2nd%20class%20lever.png)

(_Formulas:_)

IMA = Effort distance divided by load distance &rarr; D<sub>effort</sub>/D<sub>load</sub>

AMA = Force of resistance divided by force of effort &rarr; F<sub>resistance</sub>/F<sub>effort</sub>

Ideal Effort Force = Multiply the load force by the load distance, then divide by the effort distance &rarr; (F<sub>load</sub>&middot;D<sub>load</sub>)/D<sub>effort</sub>

## Third Class Levers:
![Third Class Lever](3rd%20class%20lever.png)

(_Formulas:_)

IMA = Effort distance divided by resistance distance &rarr; D<sub>effort</sub>/D<sub>resistance</sub>

AMA = Force of resistance divided by force of effort &rarr; F<sub>resistance</sub>/F<sub>effort</sub>

Ideal Effort Force = Multiply the load force by the load distance, then divide by effort distance &rarr; (F<sub>resistance</sub>&middot;D<sub>resistance</sub>)/D<sub>effort</sub>
